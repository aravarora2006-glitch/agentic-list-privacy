Privacy policy

Arav agentic list. Last updated 27 October 2026. See also the terms of service.

What this app is

Arav agentic list is a private, personal tool. It is used only by its developer, on the developer's own Google accounts, to build a daily to-do list. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up or use it.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads messages to find tasks, applies the app's own labels, and saves draft replies for the developer to review. It never sends, forwards or deletes email.
  • Google Drive (drive.readonly): reads file names, document text and comments, read only.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): reads events, read only, for one account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the developer's own computer to produce a task list for the developer.
  • To summarise and organise tasks, relevant excerpts are processed by an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) acting for the developer. They are not used to train models by this app.
  • The task list keeps titles, one-line summaries and links only, never full email bodies.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the operating system's credential store on the developer's computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or transferred except as needed to provide the features above to the developer.

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further reads. Locally stored task data can be deleted by the developer at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
